Hunza Valley Travel Guide: Mountains, Hiking and Adventure

AK
Ali Khan · 9 Oct 2026 · 7 min read
Snow peaks, turquoise lakes and roads that touch the sky.

Hunza Valley is one of the most beautiful places on earth. Surrounded by giant mountains like Rakaposhi and Ultar Sar, it is a dream destination for travellers, hikers and anyone who loves adventure. Whether you are planning a family holiday or a solo backpacking trip, this guide covers everything you need.

We spent two weeks exploring the valley, its villages and its trekking trails. Here is what we learned.

Best time to visit Hunza

April and May bring cherry and apricot blossoms, while June to September is perfect for hiking and camping. October paints the whole valley in gold and orange autumn colours. Winters are very cold, but the snow views are unforgettable.

Top adventures in the valley

  • Hike to Rakaposhi Base Camp: a full day trek with glacier views.
  • Boating at Attabad Lake: bright blue water between steep mountains.
  • Walk the Hussaini Suspension Bridge: one of the most thrilling bridges in the world.
  • Drive to Khunjerab Pass: the highest paved border crossing, at 4,693 metres.
  • Camping under the stars: the night sky here is full of stars.
Travel tip: Book your hotel and tour early in summer. The best guest houses with mountain views fill up weeks in advance.

How to get there

Most travellers fly from Islamabad to Gilgit and then drive about two hours to Karimabad, the main town of Hunza. If you enjoy road trips, the Karakoram Highway journey takes around 15 hours and is an adventure in itself.

What to pack

Bring warm layers even in summer, good hiking shoes, a power bank, sunscreen and some cash, since ATMs are limited in remote villages. A light backpack makes day hikes much easier.

Final thoughts

Hunza is not just a holiday, it is an experience that stays with you. Plan your trip, pack your bags and let the mountains do the rest.
